Tool #2

Price Per Watt: The Only Number That Matters

Monthly payments hide the truth. Price per watt is how installers actually compare quotes. Divide the price by system size and see where your quote lands.

Green: strong deal. Yellow: typical range. Orange: high — worth shopping. Red: too high unless the price includes a battery. Cash and financed quotes are different sports: dealer fees can add 15–30% to a financed system, so compare financed-to-financed and cash-to-cash.
