Meet the Founder

Early on, Joe worked for one installer. He learned the trade there, and he learned its limits.

That company did a few things really well. It didn’t do everything. Different roofs need different mounts, some bills make more sense with a battery, and not every company offers the financing that fits. When the best answer lived somewhere else, his answer had to be no.

Over 450 clients later, across Rhode Island, Massachusetts and Connecticut, Joe knows there’s more than one right way to get this done. His job is to find the one that fits you, and ours is to make it happen.
