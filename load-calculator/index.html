How this is calculated. Running watts come from the original KnowSolar load sheet. Continuous kW is quantity times running watts. Surge kW uses the starting watts. Energy per day is quantity times running watts times hours, times days per week over 7, divided by 1,000.

The right-hand column is the sized figure used for design: demand is taken at 40% of connected load (diversity), and energy is divided by 0.8 for inverter efficiency, then multiplied by 1.2 for system losses and 1.05 for a 5% margin. Estimated annual kWh assumes 182 summer days and 183 winter days.

** Fridge, freezer, central A/C and the washer cycle on and off, so the hours are run hours, not clock hours.
Note: the original calculator collected days per week but never used it. Here it is applied, so a load running 2 days a week counts as 2/7.
