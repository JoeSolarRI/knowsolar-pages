What's Different Here

South Kingstown Is Not A Generic Solar Market.

South Kingstown jobs often come with a roof that is already due, which is why we quote the removal and reinstall in the same conversation.

Rural properties

Detached garages, outbuildings and big south-facing roofs open options a suburban lot does not have.

Well and septic

Not a solar issue directly, but it changes where trenching and equipment can go. We ask before anyone shows up.

Low bills

If you heat with oil or propane, your electric bill can look small and hide a bigger total energy cost. We look at both.
